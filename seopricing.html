This is your structure, from 8 September: “structures that reward performance are better long-term because it allows agencies not to get complacent.” We agreed then and we still do.
Where it pays, and where it does not
At $20,000 of attributed revenue a month: $1,500 + 1.5% = $1,800 vs flat $2,450 you save $650 At $50,000 a month: $1,500 + 1.5% = $2,250 vs flat $2,450 you save $200 At $63,000 a month — the crossover: $1,500 + 1.5% = $2,445 vs flat $2,450 level At $100,000 a month: $1,500 + 1.5% = $3,000 vs flat $2,450 you pay $550 more At $200,000 a month: $1,500 + 1.5% = $4,500 vs flat $2,450 you pay $2,050 more

So the share costs you less while it is proving itself and more once it is working. That is the point of it, and it is the right way round — but you should see the crossover before you choose, not after.

Your Google dashboard currently shows 244 conversions with 5 leads underneath. Nobody should be paid a percentage against that, including us — which is why the performance fee does not start until the tracking does. That line is yours, from the Support page.

Option Five — Flat Monthly, No Build Fee

If you would rather not write a build cheque at all, we fold it into the monthly for the first six months. Then it drops. Same work, same scope, same start date — the only difference is how it lands on your bank statement.

Foundation Months 1–6 $1,950 / mo Month 7 onward $1,200 / mo Complete <-- the one we would pick Months 1–6 $3,850 / mo Month 7 onward $2,450 / mo Complete + Weekly Months 1–6 $4,350 / mo Month 7 onward $2,950 / mo
It is not a financing charge, and it is not dearer

Complete over six months this way comes to $23,100. Paying the build separately comes to $23,200. We rounded the monthly down rather than up, so the flat route is marginally cheaper, not more expensive.

What it actually does is move the risk. On the standard structure you pay $4,250 before we have shown you anything. On this one you pay a month at a time, and if we are not producing you stop paying and we stop working. There is no build cheque sitting with us that you cannot get back.

And it steps down rather than running forever. After month six you are on the ongoing rate for ongoing work, not still paying for a build that was finished half a year ago.

Why Six Months On Any Of Them
Because we told you the timeline before you asked for a price

Stryker Digital, whose method much of our analysis follows, publish that “meaningful Map Pack movement typically lands between day 120 and 180.” That is four to six months, stated by a firm selling the service.

We are not going to sell you a term shorter than the thing we said it takes. A three-month deal ends the week before it starts working.

What happens quickly: the fixes, the faster pages, the Chittenango page finally saying Chittenango, the instant quote, the VSL. What compounds slowly is the ranking — and that is what the six months is for.
