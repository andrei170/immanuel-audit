The honest version, and it argues against us

If your own number is right, take the flat. You worked out that closing the visibility gap is worth around $350,000 a month. At that level the share would cost you $1,500 + $5,250 = $6,750 a month against $2,450 flat. That is roughly $4,300 a month more, and over $25,000 across the six months.

So why offer it at all? Because you have been paying an agency that told you Facebook was going well when it was not. The share exists for the case where you do not yet believe the forecast — it costs you less while we are proving it and more once we have. That is the right way round, and you should see the crossover before you choose rather than after.

What never counts: referrals, word of mouth, repeat customers, and anything we cannot tie to a specific page or campaign. Attribution gets agreed in writing before we start, and no share is charged until the tracking is live and we are both reading the same dashboard.

Your Google dashboard currently reports 244 conversions with 5 leads underneath it. Nobody should be paid a percentage against numbers like that, including us. That line is yours.

Why Six Months On Any Of Them
Because we told you the timeline before you asked for a price

Stryker Digital, whose method much of our analysis follows, publish that “meaningful Map Pack movement typically lands between day 120 and 180.” That is four to six months, stated by a firm selling the service.

We are not going to sell you a term shorter than the thing we said it takes. A three-month deal ends the week before it starts working.

What happens quickly: the fixes, the faster pages, the Chittenango page finally saying Chittenango, the instant quote, the VSL. What compounds slowly is the ranking — and that is what the six months is for.

What None Of These Include

  • Google Ads management. Your Google Ads are performing and staying where they are.
