You told us the problem with your last agency was never the ads alone. Nobody reported, nobody told you what was working, and you had to chase it while running jobs.

So this page does two things. First, it sets out exactly what the support looks like — who you talk to, how often, and what lands in your inbox. Then it prices it the way you suggested: three tiers, the same work in each, separated by how much of our time you get.

In your words, 8 September
"She wasn't doing reports. It was just, just have the CRM, but we'd reach out if we had something."
"We need someone we can trust, and just like, all right, tell us what to do, we'll do it."

So support is not an extra here. It is the thing that failed. Everything below is built so you never have to go looking for what your marketing is doing.

The Support Side — What Happens Every Month
  • One named client success manager. Not a ticket queue. The same person every time, who knows your account, your service area and your numbers.
  • A report you can read in five minutes. What went well, what did not, and what we are changing next — with a short video walking through it, so you can watch it between jobs.
  • Measured on booked jobs, not clicks. Cost per qualified lead, cost per booked job, split repair against replacement, and calls counted alongside forms.
  • A direct chat line to the team. Questions answered by people working on your account, not a general inbox.
  • "Here is what we need from you" — written down. If we need photos, job details or a review pushed, we tell you exactly what and by when. You said that is how you want to work.
  • You own everything. Ad accounts, CRM, tracking and data stay in your name. If you leave, it all stays with you.
