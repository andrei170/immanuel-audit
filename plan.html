The One-Off: Fixing The Website And The Tracking
Estimate — $1,500 to $2,500, one time

This is not a new website. Yours works and we are not proposing to rebuild it. This is the list of fixes above, done once:

  • Conversion tracking attached and verified end to end — GA4, Google Ads, Meta, and call tracking so phone calls count alongside forms
  • A multi-step qualification form that separates a $1,800 repair from a $19,000 replacement before it reaches your team
  • Rating, review count and named testimonials moved onto the homepage
  • Services you do not offer removed
  • A page for Oneida
  • The customer journey simplified to a clear step-by-step

Where in the range: the bottom if it is the tracking and the form only; the top if the page work and the Oneida page are included. We would confirm the number once we have seen inside the site, not before.

This one is worth doing whatever else you decide, including staying with your current agency — because until it is done, nobody can tell you what your advertising is producing.

Why the middle one

You are already spending roughly $5,500 a month between Facebook and Google. The recommended option is not asking you to spend more — it is asking you to spend about the same, with the tracking attached, the offer changed and both channels actually running.

Option 1 is genuinely cheaper than today, and it is the honest choice if you want to prove the approach before committing. But at $2,000 of spend across two platforms, one of them is always starved, and the repair campaign — your best margin — is the one that gets cut.
