The Biggest Roofing Advertisers In America Run Dozens Of Ads At Once. Almost None Of Them Leads With A Discount.

Prepared for Isaac · Immanuel Enterprises · 14 September 2026 · AG1 Consulting

Leaf Home (formerly Erie Home) · #1 roofing contractor by revenue, 2026

You asked who is spending the most and doing it best. These are the roofing companies with the largest live ad libraries on Meta — and what they actually say.

The pattern is the same from a $2 billion company down to a single franchise owner: many ads live at once, new ones every week, and the offer is financing, a fast price, a guarantee or honesty — not money off on its own.

1 — Erie Home: Volume, Warranty, Financing
Now part of Leaf Home — $2,093,934,000 in 2026 revenue, #1 on the Top 100
"🚨ATTENTION HOMEOWNERS 🚨 We want to install Complete 'Weather-Durable' Metal Shingle Systems on homes of all shapes and sizes! Click below NOW ... to see if we're installing in your zip code." "✅ Backed with a 50-year Transferable Warranty" "💸 Ask us about $0 down financing" "💪 FREE, No-obligation inspection is available" Live ads: ~95 Most show "multiple versions" — each ad is being split-tested

The biggest roofing company in the country does not sell a price cut. It sells a warranty, a payment plan and "are we in your zip code". And it runs close to a hundred ads at once, most with several versions being tested.

2 — Mr. Roof: Make The Payment The Offer
$87M revenue, #36 on the 2026 Top 100 — 21 new ads in September alone
"Don't wait for rates to drop—we've already dropped them to zero." "🏠 0% Interest for 18 Months – Most Jobs Finished in 1 Day!" "✅ Never Overpay Promise — We'll match any lower comparable quote, guaranteed." "✅ 1-Day Installation — Most installations are completed in just 24 hours."

A $19,000 replacement is a payment problem, not a price problem. Mr. Roof turns it into "0% for 18 months" and pairs it with a price-match promise, so the discount-hunter has nothing to wait for. They do run a seasonal sale (20% off for Labor Day) — but as one ad among many, not the whole library.

~160 live ads across its franchise locations
Mighty Dog Roofing of West Pittsburgh — video ad: "My name is Matt Bielawa. I own Mighty Dog Roofing right here in Carnegie." "Now I'm telling you the same thing I told him." This is the one closest to what you described on our call. The owner, on camera, in his own town, teaching a homeowner how to read a quote. You told us you want the site to show you, your wife, your crew and the building going up in Oneida. The same footage is the best ad you could run.

5 — Roof Maxx: Win Trust By Talking People Out Of It
A different product — but the angle is worth stealing
"Most roofers make more money tearing your roof off and starting over." "We're the roofing company that will talk you OUT of a replacement if you don't need one." "Whatever your roof needs — we'll tell you the truth."

Roof Maxx sells a shingle treatment, not replacements, so do not copy the product. Copy the honesty. It is the same move as Black Label's "don't get tricked into an unnecessary $15,000 replacement" — and it fits a company whose whole identity is doing the work for the Lord.

6 — Blue Bird Roofing: The One You Already Like
17 live ads — 9 of them started since 4 September
"You meet a polished salesman, sign the contract — and then a crew of strangers you've never met shows up to do the actual work." "There's a reason 'roofer' and 'scam' show up in the same sentence so often — too many homeowners have been burned by one." "Tired of calling roofing companies just to hear, 'We'll give you a price when we get there'?" "We tore this one off and fully replaced it in a single day — premium lifetime shingles, 50-year warranty, backed by our $2,500 guarantee."

Notice what their $2,500 is. Not $2,500 off — a $2,500 guarantee. Same number as your old offer, opposite message: one says "we're cheap", the other says "we're sure".

And every other hook is a fear the homeowner already has — the salesman, the strangers, the scam, the vague price. That is why their approach feels right to you.

What We Would Run When Your Ads Come Back On
  • You and your crew on camera in Oneida — the Mighty Dog format, with the building going up behind you.
  • A guarantee, not a discount — Blue Bird's move. The number can stay; the meaning changes.
  • Financing as the offer for replacements — the Mr. Roof and Erie Home move, if you offer it.
  • The honest-repair ad for repairs — Roof Maxx and Black Label's angle, which also sorts repair from replacement before anyone calls.
  • 15–30 ads a month, rotated — so nothing runs until it is tired.
  • Only after the website and town pages are in place — as you said, SEO first, ads to maximise.
What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • How much any of them spend, or what their ads earn. Revenue figures are whole-company numbers from Roofing Contractor's 2026 Top 100 — not revenue from advertising.
  • Whether a given ad is profitable. Ads running for weeks with many versions are a sign of confidence, not proof of return.
  • Guardian Roofing was left out: several unrelated companies share the name in the library and we could not attribute the ads reliably.
  • Ad counts change daily, and Erie Home's ads also include basement and waterproofing products.

The biggest roofers are not louder about price. They are louder about trust.

Which is exactly the website and the story you described on our call.
