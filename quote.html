The Google Business Profile Condition, In Writing
We are putting this in the proposal deliberately

Additional Google Business Profiles are the largest single lever in local search and they are also the fastest way to lose the profile you already rank with.

Google's published rules allow one profile per real location where you serve customers, staffed during business hours. Two profiles at one address to occupy more map positions is a policy violation that can suspend both. Mail drops, virtual offices and unstaffed units are not eligible. Suspensions are widely reported as extremely difficult to reverse.

We will not create a profile at an address that is not genuinely staffed, and we would advise against anyone who offers to.

What we will do: set every service area on your existing profile, up to the twenty Google permits — free, and in month one. And if your new building becomes a genuinely staffed location, we will plan a second profile around it properly, verified as itself.

Room for twenty more jobs a month, and five local pages.

The crew, the trucks and the reputation are already paid for. The pages are the cheapest thing you will buy this year.
