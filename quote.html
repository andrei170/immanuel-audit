Why It Drops After Three Months
The heavy work is front-loaded and there is no honest way around that

Sixty plus pages get written once. The site gets rebuilt once. The VSL gets made once. By month four the job has changed from building to keeping it healthy, and the fee should change with it.

Most agencies charge the build rate forever. That is a large part of why your owner would rather bring this in-house, and he is not wrong to think it. We would rather tell you up front that it gets cheaper.

Your own idea, from 22 September — go intense for 90 days, then step down.

One caution, and it argues against us

Local SEO takes sixty to a hundred and fifty days. Ninety days is the early end of that. If the pages are still climbing at day ninety, easing off then could cost momentum. We would tell you that at the time rather than take the easier fee, and the choice would be yours.
