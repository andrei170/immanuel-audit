Why the price should fall, rather than us pretending the work does not

The heavy lifting is front-loaded and there is no honest way around that. Sixty pages get written once. The site gets rebuilt once. The VSL gets made once. By month four the job has changed from building to keeping it healthy, and the fee should change with it.

Most agencies charge you the build rate forever. That is a large part of why your owner would rather bring this in-house. We would rather tell you up front that it gets cheaper.

It is your choice, not automatic. If at day ninety you would rather keep going hard, we keep going hard at the full rate.

One caution, and it is against our interest to raise it

Local SEO takes sixty to a hundred and fifty days. Ninety days is the early end of that. If the pages are still climbing at day ninety, stepping down then may cost you momentum — and we would tell you that at the time rather than take the easier fee.

The honest version, and it argues against us

If the work lands the way we both expect, the flat is cheaper for you. Past roughly $63,000 a month of traced revenue the share costs you more, and it keeps climbing from there.

So why offer it at all? Because you have been paying an agency that told you Facebook was going well when it was not. The share exists for the case where you do not yet believe the forecast — it costs you less while we are proving it and more once we have. That is the right way round, and you should see the crossover before you choose rather than after.

What never counts: referrals, word of mouth, repeat customers, and anything we cannot tie to a specific page or campaign. Attribution gets agreed in writing before we start, and no share is charged until the tracking is live and we are both reading the same dashboard.

Your Google dashboard currently reports 244 conversions with 5 leads underneath it. Nobody should be paid a percentage against numbers like that, including us. That line is yours.

Why Six Months
Because we told you the timeline before you asked for a price

Stryker Digital, whose method much of our analysis follows, publish that “meaningful Map Pack movement typically lands between day 120 and 180.” That is four to six months, stated by a firm selling the service.

We are not going to sell you a term shorter than the thing we said it takes. A three-month deal ends the week before it starts working.

What happens quickly: the fixes, the faster pages, the Chittenango page finally saying Chittenango, the instant quote, the VSL. What compounds slowly is the ranking — and that is what the six months is for.

What This Does Not Include
Stated plainly so there is no argument later
  • Google Ads management. Your Google Ads are performing and staying where they are.
  • Facebook ad management or spend. You have paused it, and we would not restart until the pages and tracking are in place.
  • Video production. We script and direct the VSL; the videographer's fee is yours.
  • Additional Google Business Profiles. One profile per genuinely staffed location — we will not create one at an address that is not, and we would advise against anyone who offers to.
