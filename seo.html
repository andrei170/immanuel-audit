Finding 1 — Your Chittenango Page Is Living At The Wrong Address
URL: /service-area/cazenovia-ny-copy/ Title: "Chittenango - Immanuel Enterprises Inc" Content: 410 words, genuinely about Chittenango (only 55.4% overlap with your Cazenovia page — it is real, separate writing) Canonical: points at itself, so Google indexes it as-is
This is not a duplicate page. It is worse and better at the same time.

You have a proper Chittenango page. Somebody duplicated the Cazenovia page to build it, rewrote the content correctly — and never changed the URL. So the page is about Chittenango while its address says "cazenovia-ny-copy".

Google reads URLs as a ranking signal. You have done the hard part, the writing, and thrown away the keyword by leaving the slug. Anyone who sees that URL in a search result also reads "copy".

Fix: move it to /service-area/chittenango-ny/ and 301 redirect the old address. Small job, and it is the single highest-value thing on this page.

County pages do not win town searches

Somebody in Baldwinsville with a leak does not search "roofer Onondaga County". They search their own town, and Google strongly prefers a page that names it.

This is the number from our last call, turned around. American Remodeling has 105 town pages. Prestige has 227. You have two — and one of those is sitting at the wrong URL.

Your county pages are genuinely well written — we compared Madison against Oneida and they share only 56.7% of their wording after swapping the county name out. That is real content, not a template farm, and it proves the writing can be done properly at scale.

This is the growth. Not a rebuild, not new tracking — the same thing you have already done five times, done twenty more times.

Finding 3 — Two Service Pages For A Roofing And Siding Company
/service/roofing/ 653 words /service/siding/ 596 words No page for: roof repair · roof replacement · storm damage gutters · emergency repair · inspections commercial roofing · flat roofing
The service page and the town page multiply each other

"Roofing" and "siding" are the two broadest terms you could pick, and the hardest to rank for. "Roof repair in Canastota" is a far easier search to win and a far more urgent customer — somebody with a leak rather than somebody idly considering a new roof.

Your homepage says you serve commercial as well as residential. There is no commercial roofing page at all, which is the higher-value half of your business going unrepresented.

Finding 4 — Two Pages In Your Sitemap That Should Not Be There
/author/opunzopo/ a WordPress author archive /category/uncategorized/ the default, unrenamed blog category
Small, but they are being published to Google as pages worth indexing

Neither has any value to a customer. "Uncategorized" is the WordPress factory default — the same class of leftover as the "copy" in your Chittenango URL. Both should be excluded from the sitemap and set to noindex.

On the same theme: your contact page is 170 words, the thinnest page on the site, and it is the one a buying customer lands on last.

American Remodeling can write another hundred town pages. Prestige can write another two hundred. Neither of them can credibly become a Christian-owned business, and a homeowner searching that way has already decided what matters to them before they compare a single price.

It is also a search with almost no competition, in a market where the two leaders are fighting over the same generic roofing terms. Your company is named Immanuel. The positioning is already in the name and it appears nowhere on the website.

And the building is a trust signal with a deadline on it

You are putting up a new building. That is the single strongest proof a roofing company can offer — permanence — and it is worth more while it is happening than once it is finished. Progress photographs on an About page, dated, are evidence a storm-chaser can never produce.

Same principle on the gallery: completed jobs, with the town name in the image file and alt text. That is how a project gallery earns its keep rather than just looking nice.

Two of the three firms we studied compete on certainty, not price

An instant quote is not really about the number. It is about removing the thing homeowners dread — the salesman in the living room. It also captures the detail of the job before anyone picks up a phone, which is what turns an enquiry into something you can price.

Black Label's hook is worth sitting with too: "don't get tricked into an unnecessary $15,000 replacement" qualifies the lead and attacks the competition in one line. That is a repair-first message from a company that makes its money on replacements — and it works because it is the homeowner's actual fear.

Finding 7 — The Map Pack, And The Tactic That Gets People Suspended
Read this section twice before you act on any of it

The biggest single lever in local search is not the website at all. It is the three results in the map pack, and the firms growing fastest in home services are the ones treating additional Google Business Profiles in adjacent markets as a growth strategy.

They are right that it works. Most of the people copying them get suspended.

Google's own rules, as published: One profile per real location where you serve customers A service-area business run from one central location gets ONE profile for the whole area it serves A profile may list up to 20 service areas — cities or postal codes Two profiles at the same address to occupy more map positions policy violation — BOTH can be suspended A mail drop, virtual office or unstaffed unit not eligible unless staffed in business hours A home-based business that does not hide its address suspended eventually 2026: verification is stricter, with more profiles routed through video verification
Suspensions here are not a slap on the wrist

They are reported as very common and extremely difficult to reverse, and a suspension can take the profile you already rank with, not just the new one. For a business whose name and reputation are the product, that is not a risk worth taking for a shortcut.

We would not set this up for you on a mailbox and we would advise against anyone who offers to.

But you are in an unusually good position to do it properly
  • You are putting up a new building. A genuinely staffed premises in a different market is exactly what a second profile legitimately requires. Most firms chasing this tactic have to fake the prerequisite. You are building one. That is worth planning around now rather than after it opens — where it sits could be worth more than what it costs.
  • You can list up to twenty service areas on the profile you already have, today, free. If your current profile lists only Oneida, that is the cheapest thing on this entire document. We cannot see your profile, so this is a question for you, not a finding.
  • Town pages and the map pack reinforce each other. A Baldwinsville page gives Google a reason to show you for Baldwinsville even where your profile is not the nearest pin.
The honest order of operations

Service areas first (free, today). Town pages second (the work). A second profile only if and when the new building is a real, staffed location — and verified properly, on video, as itself.

Finding 8 — Fast Server, Heavy Page, And Nothing To Press
Measured on your homepage, 22 September 2026: Time to first byte 0.64 s — your server is fine HTML transferred 17.9 KB compressed Assets referenced 22 Total asset weight 1.77 MB Three single images: 433 KB · 430 KB · 383 KB Conversion points: Tappable phone yes — (315) 240-0887 Sticky call bar on mobile not found Enquiry form none found on any page Contact page length 170 words Instant quote / estimator none
The problem is not your host. It is three photographs.

Your server answers in two-thirds of a second, which is better than most. Then the page pulls 1.77 MB behind it, and three images account for roughly a quarter of that on their own. On a phone, on a rural Central New York signal, that is the difference between a page that feels instant and one somebody abandons.

Properly compressed, those three images should be a fraction of their current size with no visible loss. It is a one-afternoon job and it is the cheapest speed win available.

And the harder question: what does a visitor actually press?

A tappable number is good and you have one. But there is no sticky call bar following the visitor down the page on mobile, no form we could find anywhere, and no instant quote — while two of the three competitors we studied lead with exactly that.

Somebody who is not ready to phone a stranger has nothing to do but leave. That is the whole cost of having no second option.

Finding 9 — The Search Box Is Not The Only Thing Reading Your Site Now
Worth getting ahead of rather than catching up on

A growing share of "who should I call" questions are answered by AI assistants and by Google's own AI summaries, which read a page rather than ranking a list of links. What they can quote, they can recommend.

You are unusually well set up for this and may not realise it. Your structured data already declares RoofingContractor, opening hours, address, aggregate rating and individual reviews — that is precisely the machine-readable material these systems lean on, and most of your competitors do not have it.

What is missing is the plain-language answers they prefer to quote: a real FAQ, "what does a roof replacement cost in Central New York", "how do I know if I need repair or replacement", "what happens after storm damage". Written as direct answers, not as a brochure. The same content also serves the repair and replacement split you already wanted.

The Roadmap, In Four Phases
PHASE 1 — Fix what is already built Week 1 Move the Chittenango page to /service-area/chittenango-ny/ and 301 the old URL Compress the three 400 KB images Noindex /author/opunzopo/ and /category/uncategorized/, drop both from the sitemap Set every service area on the Google Business Profile (up to 20 — free, today) PHASE 2 — Build the front door Weeks 2 to 5 An enquiry form on every page, asking roof age, repair or replacement, town, urgency, insurance claim or not A sticky call bar on mobile An instant quote or online estimate Why Immanuel · What to Expect · a rebuilt Contact page Repair and replacement split into separate pages PHASE 3 — Scale the pages Weeks 4 to 14 Town pages, written not templated, largest and nearest first: Canastota · Oneida · Utica · Rome · Syracuse · Baldwinsville · Manlius · Fayetteville · Liverpool · DeWitt Service pages: roof repair · storm damage · emergency · gutters · commercial Then the combinations: roof repair in Canastota Gallery, with town names in filenames and alt text FAQ answers written for AI assistants to quote PHASE 4 — Expand Month 4 onward Review velocity, deliberately and continuously Progress photographs of the new building while it goes up A second Google Business Profile — ONLY if the new building is a genuinely staffed location, verified as itself
What to expect, stated honestly, before you spend anything

Local search does not move in a fortnight. Anyone who tells you rankings shift in 30 days is selling you something. In this trade, meaningful movement in local and map results typically shows somewhere around the four to six month mark, and it compounds after that.

We are telling you that now, because the alternative is you judging the work at week six and concluding it failed. Phases 1 and 2 produce visible change quickly — a faster page, a form that captures jobs, a Chittenango page that finally says Chittenango. Phase 3 is where the compounding starts and it needs patience.

You have already paid for the hard part. Nobody has written the pages.
